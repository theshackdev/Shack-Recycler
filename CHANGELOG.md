# Changelog

## 1.1.1
- A Shack Workbench Pack backpack with a stonecutter packed in gives back the stonecutter's materials too (Wood 10, Iron 2, Stone 4 in the base game), not only the backpack's.
- Works together with 1.1.0.

## 1.1.0
- **New: Research tab** at the workbench. Research something you can't craft yet: each research uses it up (nothing comes back; stacks use one craft's worth). After 5 researches you learn its recipe. Food is included; a mead teaches its mead base, and oven food teaches its dough. Progress is saved on your character.
- **New setting, on by default: Only known recipes.** You can only recycle things you know how to craft; others show greyed out as "not known". This stops recycling from handing out materials from tiers you haven't reached.
- New settings: Research Enabled, Stations, Researches needed, Excluded item types.
- **The server and players need the same version** (1.1.x). 1.0.x and 1.1.x can't be mixed, so update both.

## 1.0.0
- First release. A Recycle tab at the workbench, forge and stonecutter: gear (upgrades included), tools, arrows, bolts, bombs, nails, bronze and more back into materials. Flat 100% by default, or luck rolls with a Recycling skill. Never more than an item cost to make. Gear asks Yes / No first.
